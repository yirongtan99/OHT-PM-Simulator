## Slide 15

# Current Manual Process Challenges
PAIN POINTS IN THE EXISTING METHOD
The current workflow is effective, but it is highly dependent on manual execution.
Key challenges
- Repeated plugging and unplugging between ports
- Manual panel / reflective target shifting
- Repeated checking of G / NG result
- Manual sensor adjustment if out of specification
- Manual screenshot capture and upload
- Process consistency depends heavily on technician skill and alignment accuracy
Impact
- Time-consuming PM process
- Repetitive work for line support technicians
- Possibility of variation between operators
- Difficult to scale if PM demand increases
- Manual documentation adds extra workload

PROPOSED SOLUTION
Integrate a Collaborative Robot (Cobot) arm to automatically handle the sensor data cable plug connection to the OHT.
GOAL
Reduce operational downtime, eliminate manual alignment errors and enable a fully automated, vision-assisted PM workflow.

# Connection: Magnetic Plug & Relay Station
ON THE OHT — RELAY SWITCH — PCB
Relay · VHL — VHL Sensor · 10-pin port
Min. 12-PIN MAGNETIC PLUG on the OHT
MAGNETIC PLUG at the workstation
Laptop/Desktop Workstation
Relay · OBS Right — 2-PIN JUMPER — OBS Right · 10-pin port
Relay · OBS Left — OBS Left · 10-pin port
Relay · OBS Center — OBS Center · 10-pin port
Switch selects one sensor at a time
5 of 10 pins used each
Inside each 10-pin port — only 5 pins used
How it works
A single 12-pin magnetic plug on the OHT carries all four sensors' signals (3 each). At the workstation, a 2-pin jumper feeds a relay-switch bank that connects one sensor at a time.
This reproduces the manual plug-in / plug-out routine from one fixed station, no repeated unplugging between ports.
3 signal pins — a unique colour per sensor
2 jumper pins (blue)
5 pins unused

## Slide 23

# The Verdict
OMRON (TM5S) — NOT SELECTED
Pro: Excellent out-of-the-box vision integration
Con: Severely breaks the budget at >SGD 100k
Con: Only saves 10 min per PM — weak cost-to-benefit
ABB Series ★ RECOMMENDED
Pro: Highly cost-effective ~SGD 20K
Pro: Flexible 6-axis, configurable functions
Con: Needs 3rd-party vision plugin (e.g., SICK)
RECOMMENDED PATH
The current 4-port plug is too difficult for basic vision systems. Purchase a cost-effective ABB / UR cobot and retrofit the fleet with magnetic plugs (alignment by feel), accounting for the one-time ~SGD 7,344 manpower retrofit cost.
